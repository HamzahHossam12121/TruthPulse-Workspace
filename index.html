About the project

Why we built TruthPulse

Misleading media spreads faster than ever — and AI-generated content is getting harder to spot. Our project gives everyday users a simple, transparent tool to check what they are seeing.

Analyze

Upload an image, video, or file. The planned AI model will review it for signs of manipulation or synthetic origin.

Ask questions

The planned chat will explain results in plain language, rather than showing a score without context.

Keep your history

Once accounts and backend storage are connected, you will be able to return to previous analyses.

About the team

Built by students, for everyone

We are a small international student team competing in this innovation challenge. Our goal is to make media verification understandable and accessible — not to replace professional fact-checkers, but to help regular users make more informed decisions.

  • Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Bootstrap 5
  • Backend: Python + a custom AI model
  • Focus: transparency, clarity, and user trust

"We want users to understand why something looks suspicious — not just see a red warning label."

— The TruthPulse team
